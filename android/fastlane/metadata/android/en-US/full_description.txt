Danaview is the modern, ultra-high performance HDR media player built for speed, fidelity, and versatility. Powered by an advanced hardware-accelerated playback engine, Danaview plays virtually any video format, audio stream, and subtitle file with silky-smooth frame rates and zero battery waste.

Whether you're playing high-bitrate HEVC and AV1 movies from your local storage, streaming live HLS/DASH broadcasts, or opening videos directly from your file manager, Danaview delivers an uncompromising cinematic experience.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:

UNIVERSAL HIGH-DEFINITION VIDEO ENGINE
• Hardware-accelerated decoding for HEVC/H.265, AV1, H.264/AVC, VP9, and ProRes.
• Wide container support: MKV, MP4, WebM, AVI, MOV, TS, and FLV.
• True HDR10, HLG, and wide color gamut rendering.

STUDIO-GRADE AUDIO CONTROLS
• 10-band parametric equalizer with studio presets (Rock, Pop, Classical, Flat, and Custom).
• Real-time Bass Boost and dynamic volume normalization for clear dialogue.
• Multi-track audio selection supporting AAC, AC3, E-AC3, TrueHD, FLAC, and Opus.

REAL-TIME STREAM DIAGNOSTICS
• Built-in network speed test and ping analyzer for stream readiness.
• Live diagnostics overlay: dropped frames, buffer health, current bitrate, and decoder status.
• Fast link validation for direct HTTP, HTTPS, and HLS/DASH network streams.

REAL-TIME COLOR GRADING & FILTERS
• Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, and gamma during live playback.
• A-B loop repeat for studying scenes, lectures, or music videos.
• Aspect ratio options: fit, stretch, crop, and cinematic widescreen zoom.

INTUITIVE GESTURE CONTROLS
• Swipe vertically on the left for brightness; swipe on the right for volume.
• Horizontal swipes for precision seek with visual thumbnails and configurable seek steps.
• Screen lock prevents accidental touches during playback.

PRIVACY FIRST & ON-DEVICE
• 100% on-device sandbox. No telemetry, no tracking scripts, no ads.
• Background audio playback with notification and media control support.
