# Danaview brand sheet

The mark is **6B, the page-fold Dv**: a solid white D-shaped page whose top-right corner is folded in. The two free edges of the fold form the **v**. A page reads as any media (a frame, a photo, a document or a canvas), and the fold reads as editing. The visual version of this sheet is `brand_sheet.png`. Every asset here is generated by `generate_brand.py`.

## Construction (1024 grid, centred on 512, 512)

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| Page | 448 square, x and y 288 to 736 |
| Spine corners | r 68 (top-left and bottom-left) |
| Bowl | bottom-right arc, r 216, centred on (520, 520) |
| Fold | 35° line from A (516, 288), 220 in from the corner, to B (736, 442), 154 down (220 × tan 35°) |
| Flap | the corner reflected across AB, with its vertex C at (591.2, 494.7). The free edges AC and CB form the v: 90° open, tilted 10° |
| Knockout | the page is cut 44 around the flap. The flap's fold edge is set 20 in from AB and drawn with a 4 round stroke, leaving a **40 visible gap** |
| Safe areas | the mark sits within r 296 of the centre. That is inside the iOS 80% square and the 66% circle (r 338). On Android the adaptive foreground draws the mark at 2/3 scale, well inside the 66 dp safe zone |

The icon background is a linear gradient from #7C7FF5 to #5141DD (top-left to bottom-right), plus a white radial glow centred at (225, 143) with r 799, fading from 10% through 3.5% to 0. The page carries a soft drop shadow: blur 25.6, offset y 15.4, black at 16%.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep ¼ of the tile side clear on every edge. In a lockup, the gap between the mark and the word is ⅓ of the tile side.
- **Icon:** 16 px minimum. At 16 px the flap tone carries the v; the gap becomes visible from about 29 px.
- **Mono glyph:** 12 px minimum.
- **Lockup:** 24 px minimum mark height.

## Wordmark

"Danaview" in **Figtree 700**, the app font, with letter-spacing −1%. The lockups are in `wordmark/`, horizontal and stacked, each with a `_light` version that has dark text (#0F1118). They are SVG text with a system sans fallback, because no font file is embedded. **Outlined paths are not included:** they would need the Figtree font file, which is not in the repo and could not be downloaded. Outline the text once Figtree is available, before using the wordmark in print or anywhere the font is not installed.

## Do

- Use the master icon on the indigo gradient, and the mono glyph anywhere a single colour is needed.
- Keep the 35° fold, the 40 gap and the proportions exactly as generated.
- Put the product accent on the flap only.

## Don't

- Rotate, skew or stretch the mark, or redraw the fold at another angle.
- Recolour the page, or use the accents on the tile or the page.
- Close the knockout gap, or add outlines, a play triangle or other symbols.
- Place the master tile on busy imagery without its clear space.
