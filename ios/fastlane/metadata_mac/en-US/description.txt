Danaview is the modern, ultra-high performance HDR media player built for macOS. Engineered with a hardware-accelerated playback engine, Danaview effortlessly handles virtually any video format, container, subtitle, and audio codec with silky-smooth frame pacing and minimal CPU utilization.

Whether playing high-bitrate HEVC and AV1 movies from your local drive, streaming live HLS and DASH broadcasts, or managing video playlists, Danaview delivers an uncompromising cinematic experience on your Mac.

KEY FEATURES:

UNIVERSAL HIGH-DEFINITION & HDR PLAYBACK
• Native hardware-accelerated video decoding optimized for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
• Flawless support for MKV, MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI, TS, and FLV containers.
• Support for HEVC/H.265, AV1, H.264/AVC, VP9, and ProRes codecs.
• True HDR10, HLG, and wide color playback with accurate color reproduction.

REAL-TIME COLOR GRADING & VIDEO FILTERS
• Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, and gamma during live playback.
