Danaview is the modern, ultra-high performance HDR media player designed for iPhone and iPad. Engineered with a hardware-accelerated playback engine, Danaview effortlessly handles virtually any video format, container, subtitle, and audio codec with silky-smooth frame pacing and zero battery drain.

Whether playing high-bitrate HEVC and AV1 movies from your local storage, streaming live HLS and DASH broadcasts, or opening media directly from the Files app, Danaview delivers an uncompromising cinematic experience.

KEY FEATURES:

UNIVERSAL HIGH-DEFINITION & HDR PLAYBACK
• Native hardware-accelerated video decoding for HEVC/H.265, AV1, H.264/AVC, VP9, and ProRes.
• Flawless support for MKV, MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI, TS, and FLV containers.
• True HDR10, HLG, and Dolby Vision playback with accurate color reproduction.

STUDIO-GRADE AUDIO ENGINE
• 10-band parametric equalizer with custom presets (Rock, Pop, Classical, Flat, and Custom).
• Real-time Bass Boost and dynamic audio normalization for crystal-clear dialogue and punchy bass.
• Multi-track audio selection with support for AAC, AC3, E-AC3, TrueHD, FLAC, and Opus.

REAL-TIME STREAM DIAGNOSTICS
• Integrated network speed test and ping latency analyzer.
• Live playback telemetry: dropped frames, buffer health, current bitrate, and decoder status.
• Fast link validation for direct HTTP, HTTPS, and HLS/DASH media streams.

REAL-TIME COLOR GRADING & VIDEO FILTERS
• Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, and gamma during live playback.
• A-B loop repeat for studying scenes, choreography, or language learning.
• Flexible aspect ratio scaling: fit, stretch, crop, and 16:9 / 21:9 zoom.

INTUITIVE GESTURE CONTROLS
• Swipe vertically on the left to adjust brightness; swipe on the right to control volume.
• Horizontal swipes for precision seek with visual thumbnails and configurable seek steps.
• Double-tap to pause or resume; one-tap screen lock prevents accidental touches.

NATIVE FILES APP & PRIVACY FIRST
• Full iOS Files app integration: Open with Danaview directly from Files, iCloud Drive, or USB drives.
• Zero telemetry, zero tracking, and no external tracking scripts. All your data stays strictly on your device.
• Background audio playback with full lock screen and Control Center media integration.
